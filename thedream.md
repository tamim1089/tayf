i want this dream to come true 

A 10-centimeter cube you set on a chair. It watches you with its own cameras, sends you across the world, and at the other end an identical cube makes you appear — life-size, photoreal, standing or sitting in the air of that room — with no screen, no headset, no glasses, and nothing else to buy. Distance stops mattering. You are simply in the room together.

The 10 Rules

1. Free-space. The person must appear in open air, with no screen, panel, mist, or surface of any kind carrying the image.
2. No wearables. The viewer wears nothing — no headset, no glasses, no contacts, no tracking markers.
3. Life-size. The person appears at true human scale, not a figurine, icon, or miniature.
4. In the viewer's own space. The person occupies the room with the viewer — in and around the chair — not behind a window, portal, or aperture plane.
5. Photoreal. It is recognizably that specific person — their face, their expression, their identity — not a wireframe, silhouette, or avatar.
6. Ten centimeters. The device is a ~10×10×10 cm object you can pick up and set down anywhere.
7. Symmetric. Both ends are the same device; every cube both captures and displays, in both directions at once.
8. Self-contained. Nothing else to buy, install, or mount — no projector, no screen, no retroreflector, no room modification.
9. Any ordinary room. It works on a normal chair in a normal home, in normal light, with no special wall, surface, or infrastructure.
10. Safe and live. Safe to sit beside at face height indefinitely, and fast enough that conversation feels natural — under ~150 ms.
